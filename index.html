About the Project

Purpose, operational problem and solution approach

Asset Transfer Lifecycle (ATLM) is an operational monitoring solution designed to provide end-to-end visibility into asset transfer processes. The project combines data processing, reconciliation rules, SLA monitoring and risk classification to centralize operational information and identify exceptions throughout the transfer lifecycle.

SITUATION

Operational Visibility

Asset transfers involve multiple stages, teams and custodians. Without centralized monitoring, pending operations, SLA breaches, reconciliation differences and operational risks can be difficult to identify and prioritize.

TASK

Centralize & Control

The objective was to create a solution capable of consolidating transfer information, monitoring the operational lifecycle and providing a clear view of exceptions, deadlines, reconciliation results and risk.

ACTION

Data-Driven Monitoring

I developed an end-to-end monitoring solution using Python, Pandas, SQL and JavaScript , transforming operational data into structured controls and an interactive dashboard.

The solution tracks lifecycle stages, SLA performance, reconciliation results, exceptions and risk levels from request through settlement and reconciliation.

RESULT

Prioritization & Control

The solution creates a centralized operational view that helps identify transfers requiring attention, prioritize critical cases and improve visibility over the transfer lifecycle.
