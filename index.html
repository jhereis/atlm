About the Project

Operational problem, solution approach and results

Asset Transfer Lifecycle (ATLM) is an operational monitoring solution designed to provide end-to-end visibility across asset transfer processes, from request to settlement and reconciliation.

SITUATION

Limited Operational Visibility

Asset transfers involve multiple stages, teams and custodians. Pending operations, SLA breaches, reconciliation differences and operational exceptions can be difficult to identify and prioritize without centralized monitoring.

TASK

Centralize Operational Control

The objective was to consolidate transfer information and create a single operational view capable of monitoring lifecycle stages, deadlines, exceptions, reconciliation and operational risk.

ACTION

Build a Data-Driven Solution

I developed an end-to-end monitoring solution using Python, Pandas, SQL and JavaScript. Operational data is processed through validation, reconciliation, SLA and risk controls before being presented in an interactive dashboard.

RESULT

Visibility, Prioritization & Control

The solution provides a centralized view of the transfer lifecycle, helping identify operations requiring attention and prioritize exceptions and critical cases.
