About the Project

SMART framework — project objective and operational value

Asset Transfer Lifecycle (ATLM) is an operational monitoring solution designed to provide end-to-end visibility across asset transfer processes, from request to settlement and reconciliation.
SPECIFIC

Centralize Asset Transfers

Consolidate transfer information into a single operational view, covering lifecycle stages, custodians, settlement, reconciliation, exceptions and operational risk.

MEASURABLE

Monitor Operational KPIs

Track measurable indicators such as total transfers, pending operations, SLA breaches, exceptions, critical risk and requested asset value.

ACHIEVABLE

Build With Data & Automation

Develop the solution using Python, Pandas, SQL and JavaScript, applying data processing, validation, reconciliation, SLA controls and risk classification.

RELEVANT

Improve Operational Control

Provide greater visibility into pending operations, exceptions and reconciliation differences, supporting prioritization and reducing the risk of operational issues remaining unidentified.

TIME-BOUND

Control Deadlines & SLA

Monitor expected settlement dates, elapsed days and SLA status throughout the transfer lifecycle, allowing operations outside the expected timeframe to be identified and prioritized.
